Autonomous Lawn Mower Robot

Autonomy in progress

Self-built platform · ROS · RTAB-Map SLAM · reinforcement learning · embedded control

A working outdoor robot I designed, built and wired myself — chassis, drive system, power and control electronics. The hardware runs. The interesting problem is what comes next: getting it to navigate an outdoor space reliably enough to leave alone.

  • Built: mechanical platform, drive and power electronics, control wiring — maintained as a live prototype.
  • In development: navigation stack on ROS with RTAB-Map for mapping and localisation.
  • Exploring: reinforcement learning for coverage path planning — mowing every square metre once, efficiently.
  • The real lesson: outdoor failure modes have almost nothing to do with the algorithm — uneven terrain, glare, and how completely a space changes between seasons.

Green Hydrogen & Fuel Cell Study

Study stage

Technical & economic feasibility · system sizing

Surplus renewable power is worth very little at the moment it is generated and a great deal six hours later. This study looks at whether an electrolyser and fuel cell can bridge that gap.

  • Coupling an electrolyser and fuel cell to surplus renewable and waste-to-energy generation to store and firm intermittent supply.
  • Technology selection and indicative sizing against available surplus power and water.
  • The conditions under which a demonstration unit stops being a science project and starts making economic sense.
